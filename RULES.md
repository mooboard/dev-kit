# Rules

- 128×32, every pixel is an LED. Black is off.
- Keep 1 dark LED clear at every edge.
- No cut-off text and no "…". Peeking on purpose is fine.
- Animate at the board's frame rate (33 ms frames). Nothing jumps or flickers.
- Very dim colors don't show. The previewer flags them.
- The Matrix Cow is the only cow.
- American English, short labels, no semicolons or em dashes.
